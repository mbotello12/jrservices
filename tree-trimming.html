Service

Our services include precision tree trimming and pruning to enhance growth and aesthetics, strategic branch removal to eliminate hazards, and complete tree removal for dead, diseased, or obstructive trees. We also offer emergency storm damage clean-up and stump grinding, ensuring your property is left clean and clear.

When Customers Need This Service:

Customers often need tree trimming when branches are growing too close to their home or power lines, when trees appear overgrown or unhealthy, or when dead or damaged limbs pose a safety risk. Tree removal becomes necessary for hazardous trees, those blocking construction, or trees damaged beyond repair. If you're dealing with storm debris, preparing land for a new project, or simply want to improve your property's appearance and safety, our expert tree services are essential.

Why Choose This Company:

With 17 years of specialized field experience, J&R brings unparalleled expertise to every tree trimming and removal project. We prioritize safety for your property and our team, employing precise techniques to ensure efficient and clean results. Our owner-operator, James Mills, personally oversees each job, guaranteeing a strong work ethic, meticulous attention to detail, and a commitment to honest, fair pricing. Choose J&R for reliable, professional tree service that protects your investment and enhances your landscape.
